Model boundary

Fixed 15-second steps; identical exogenous demand for each policy at a given seed. Tracks use conservative exclusive directed blocks plus 90-second departure separation. Terminal turnaround is two minutes. No signalling, braking curves, junctions, station platform capacity, train failures, transfers or certified safety assessment are modelled. Optimisation is scalar, not a Pareto solver. Hold is an alighting-dependent dwell extension. Completed journeys, boarded-only wait and unfinished passengers are reported separately.
